Relationship to the official examination

NOOPIK is an independent Korean-learning app. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or certified by the National Institute for International Education (NIIED) or the official TOPIK examination. The TOPIK name describes the subject of study. Practice questions are not provided as official past examination questions, and passing is not guaranteed.

References for Korean headwords and study levels

Some Korean headwords, parts of speech and study classifications use the National Institute of Korean Language’s “Korean Vocabulary List for Learners” (2003) and vocabulary-level materials from its 2017 study on applying the International Standard Curriculum of Korean Language, Stage 4, by Kim Jung-seop and 13 other authors. These materials are used under Korea Open Government License (KOGL) Type 1 (attribution). NOOPIK removes duplicates, adjusts notation and classifies and displays the materials for study. The sources cover different scopes; not every word in the app is guaranteed to correspond directly to an official level.

  • NIKL: 2017 research report and vocabulary-level materials
  • KOGL license conditions: Type 1

Dictionary-derived Japanese meanings and reuse

Japanese meanings in supplementary vocabulary use text from NIKL’s Korean–Japanese Learners’ Dictionary (Korean Basic Dictionary). Meanings have been selected and shortened for study, with notation adjustments, additions and corrections. The identified meanings and adaptations are provided under CC BY-SA 2.0 KR (Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0 Korea). Copying, redistribution and adaptation, including commercial use, are permitted subject to attribution, the same-license requirement and the other license conditions.

This scope covers the Japanese meaning fields (meaning_ja) of 4,553 NIKL 2017 supplementary entries and 665 public-vocabulary supplementary entries: 5,218 entries in total. It includes manually supplemented or corrected meanings; it does not mean that every meaning is identical to the dictionary original. Headwords and app word IDs identify the covered fields. The license applies to these identified meanings and adaptations, not uniformly to all NOOPIK original questions, original examples, programs, images or audio.

Historical reference material

Trilingual Tomi’s intermediate TOPIK vocabulary list is a historical reference. Current Japanese meanings for the corresponding TOPIK I and II supplementary vocabulary were newly written and reviewed; they are not a redistribution of the historical Japanese comparison list.
