NOOPIK Support

Updated: October 10, 2026

For questions about learning content, bugs, subscriptions or personal information, email support@noopik.app.

What to include

Tell us your app version, device and OS, when and where the problem occurred, and steps to reproduce it. Include the question code for content issues. Do not send passwords, verification codes or card numbers. Hide unnecessary personal information in screenshots. If attachments cannot be checked, we may ask you to describe the issue in the message instead.

Subscriptions, trials and cancellation

Registering or opening the app does not start a subscription. Check trial eligibility, duration and the post-trial price on the store purchase screen. To avoid renewal of an Apple trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. On Google Play, cancel before the next billing time and check the store terms. Deleting the app or account does not cancel a subscription.

Cancel an Apple subscription · Cancel a Google Play subscription

Restoring purchases and checking charges

Sign in with the store account and NOOPIK account used for the purchase, then try restoring purchases in the app. If access is not restored, do not purchase again. Send us the purchase date, product name and displayed error. The store determines refund eligibility and processing.

Request an Apple refund · Google Play refund guidance

Reset study records on this device

Select “Reset study records on this device” in Settings. This resets learning; it does not delete your account or cancel your store subscription. Records on other devices remain and may merge back with your account history when you resume learning. Read the confirmation before continuing.

Delete your account and server data

Select “Delete account and server data” in Settings and follow the identity-verification steps. If an external-service action remains unfinished, do not assume deletion is complete; check the error. Contact support if you cannot use this option. We do not disclose account information before verifying identity or delete accounts based solely on an email sender display. Cancel store subscriptions separately.

Delete support emails

Deleting your app account may not delete every unlinked support email or record in a separate support mailbox. To request deletion, provide your sender address and approximate contact date. After identity verification, we check the records and retention needs and explain the outcome and any legal or backup exceptions. Cloudflare handles email delivery and Supabase manages the support inbox. See the Privacy Policy.

Usage analytics and error reporting

These are off by default. You can opt in in Settings in a supported updated version. Declining does not change eligibility for paid features. Turning analytics off stops new collection and transmission and deletes the unsent local analytics queue and analytics ID. See the Privacy Policy for data already transmitted.

Changing devices

Signing into the same NOOPIK account restores supported settings and a size-limited learning snapshot. This is not a complete backup of every local record; unsynchronized changes may not all be recoverable.

Audio does not play

Check device volume and the selected audio output, including Bluetooth. If the issue remains, send the question code, screen and device information.

Current availability

The current general-release version does not offer paid instructor correction or full mock exams. For a correction purchase from an earlier test version, include the purchase date and product name.

Relationship to TOPIK

NOOPIK is an independent study app, not affiliated with official TOPIK institutions. It does not provide official past exam papers or guarantee a pass or improved scores.
