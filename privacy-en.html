Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

NOOPIK requires sign-in with an email address or an Apple, Google or LINE account to use its subscription service. You enter a nickname during registration. NOOPIK does not directly request or store card details. Apple App Store or Google Play checks your payment method when you start a 7-day free trial or subscription.

1. Information stored on your device

Detailed study progress, correct and incorrect answers, study statistics, saved words, notes and notification settings are stored locally on your device or browser. To restore and synchronize learning across devices signed into your account, we also store a size-limited learning snapshot with your account ID on the server. The snapshot includes answer results, reviews, exposure history, statistics, saved words, highlights and personal notes. Supported versions also synchronize deletion markers to prevent deleted items from returning on another device. Do not put passwords, card numbers or unnecessary personal information in personal notes. Original writing answers are not included in the learning snapshot unless you explicitly request paid correction.

2. Learning accounts and settings synchronization

When you sign in with email, Apple, Google or LINE, we store your Supabase user ID, sign-in method, email address where provided, chosen nickname, assessed level, target level, exam date, language and onboarding status on the server. Supabase Auth processes email passwords as encrypted authentication information; NOOPIK operators cannot see the original password. NOOPIK does not collect or store your Apple, Google or LINE password. LINE sign-in uses the openid and profile scopes. We use your LINE user identifier, display name and profile-image URL, where available, for Supabase authentication and account-link verification. We do not request permission to access your LINE email address.

3. Information collected for paid writing correction

The current general-release version does not offer paid writing correction. Only if a future, separate version offers it and you explicitly request it will we collect the writing task and answer, account ID, language, App Store or Google Play product and purchase identifiers, purchase-verification information, request status and instructor feedback. We do not collect card details.

4. Information sent with a question report

When you report a question, we send your account ID, question code, the currently displayed question, options, answer, explanation and listening script, assessed level, content version, report category and the text you enter. Report tickets do not include your name, email address or phone number. To prevent repeated reports, identifiers for the app installation and connection network are stored as irreversible hashes. Reports are used only to check and improve content and prevent abuse. They do not automatically change the original question.

5. Purposes and service providers

Collected information is used only for sign-in and settings synchronization, purchase verification, answer delivery, instructor correction, viewing results, handling question reports, customer support, abuse prevention, service stability and improving the study flow. We use Supabase for server storage and email-account authentication; Apple, Google and LINE for social sign-in; Apple App Store or Google Play for payment; and RevenueCat to verify purchase status. We use PostHog for operational analytics. Only app launches, completed registration, level tests, study starts, whether answers are correct and reviews are used, completion counts, study streaks, and the location and type of errors are sent with a random installation analytics ID. Analytics events do not include email addresses, nicknames, account IDs, original question, option, answer or explanation text, audio or photos. We do not use PostHog person profiles, automatic capture or session replay, and disable IP-based location enrichment for analytics events. Apple or Google handles card details; NOOPIK and RevenueCat do not receive them.

6. Advertising and tracking

On the Free plan, Google AdMob rewarded ads are offered only when you choose additional learning. Ads are not shown if they are not configured or are unavailable. We do not offer forced interstitial ads or banners, and Pro does not show study ads. We request non-personalized ads and do not request tracking across other companies’ apps or websites. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing. Google Mobile Ads SDK may process your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and error and performance information for ad delivery, abuse prevention and diagnostics. NOOPIK does not include your email address, nickname or original answer, question or explanation text in ad requests, and does not sell collected information to data brokers. Depending on your region and consent status, we show Google’s privacy-consent screen. Ad privacy settings appear in Settings when changes are available.

To enforce Free-plan limits and prevent duplicate ad rewards, your device stores a hashed account identifier, the date in Japan time, remaining usage by area, used content IDs and reward identifiers. In versions with device-only allowances, these records apply to the current device and are not synchronized across devices. They are kept separately so signing out or resetting study records does not grant usage again. Account deletion removes the associated records on this device. Recovery of device-only records after reinstalling the app or changing devices is not guaranteed.

In versions with server-managed allowances enabled, the server also stores your account ID, the date in Japan time, study area, used content IDs, usage, reward-request and transaction identifiers, reward amounts and processing dates and times. These records enforce limits and prevent duplicate rewards across devices signed into the same account. For server-verified rewarded ads, we send your account ID, a randomly generated reward-request identifier and the study area to Google so the signed reward can be associated with the correct account and request. The account ID is not your email address, but it identifies your NOOPIK account. Server allowance records are included in account-deletion processing. The ad provider’s policies govern its data processing and retention.

Usage analytics and error reporting are off by default. Optional consent settings are available in updated versions that support them. If you cannot see the settings, check for an app update or contact support. We collect and send the analytics events above only after you opt in in Settings. Declining does not change your eligibility for paid features. You can turn analytics off in Settings at any time. This stops new collection and transmission and removes the unsent analytics queue and installation analytics ID from your device. This action does not immediately delete events already sent to the server. Analytics does not send original error messages or stack traces.

7. Retention and deletion

Local study information is kept until you select “Reset study records on this device” in Settings or delete the app. Pending anonymous analytics events are kept on your device for no more than 64 events and 7 days, and removed from the device queue after transmission. Resetting local records does not delete your server account or correction history. You can request account deletion through “Delete account and server data.” After any necessary identity verification, we proceed with deleting the server sign-in account, settings, learning snapshots, correction requests and results, and account data on this device. We also check required external-service actions, including deletion of RevenueCat customer information and unlinking Apple sign-in. The procedure may not finish depending on your app version or external-service status. If an error occurs or you want to confirm the deletion scope, contact support@noopik.app. After identity verification, support will explain remaining actions and progress. Payment records subject to legal retention requirements may be kept for the required period.

8. Device permissions

We request notification permission only when you enable study notifications. If you choose to attach a photo on a screen, we use the selected photo to handle that request. Do not send photos containing passwords, card numbers or unnecessary personal information. You can change permissions in your device settings at any time.

9. Privacy and deletion support

If you cannot delete data in the app or have a privacy question, contact support@noopik.app.

10. Operator

Service operator: NOOPIK operations team. Contact: support@noopik.app.

11. Customer-support email

If you contact support@noopik.app, we process your sender email address, display name, subject, message, receipt time and reply history. Cloudflare handles incoming and outgoing email, and Supabase stores the administrator support inbox. The regular support inbox stores attachment counts, not attachment contents. If a message is forwarded to a separate support mailbox because receipt failed, a size limit was exceeded or another issue arose, the original email, including attachments, may be kept there. Include only information needed to resolve your issue. Do not send passwords, verification codes or card numbers.

Support records are retained while needed to handle and follow up on your request. After resolution, we review their necessity and delete unnecessary personal information. Email that is not automatically linked to your account, or records in a separate support mailbox, may not all be deleted by deleting your app account. To request deletion of support records, provide the sender address and approximate contact date. We verify identity separately before disclosing or deleting account-related personal information; we do not rely only on the email sender display.

12. External-service retention, deletion and backups

Server accounts and learning snapshots are retained as needed to provide the service and handled in response to account-deletion requests. Non-identifying content-improvement records in question reports may be retained separately from account deletion. Events already sent to PostHog, Cloudflare processing records, separate support mailboxes and Apple or Google purchase and refund records are subject to each provider’s retention policies and legal obligations. The 7-day limit for the device queue is not a retention limit for all external servers. Contact support to confirm applicable retention periods and deletion assistance. Recovery backups are managed separately from active data and may remain until their retention cycle ends. Deleting your account or the app does not cancel a store subscription.
